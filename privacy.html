Privacy Policy — Duplicate Image Remover

Last updated: September 2026

Duplicate Image Remover ("the app") is a Reddit app built on Devvit that detects and removes duplicate image posts within a subreddit. This policy explains what data it processes and why.

What the app accesses

When a new image post is submitted in a subreddit where the app is installed, the app:

  • Downloads the image from Reddit's own image hosting (i.redd.it) or Imgur, in order to analyze it.
  • Computes a short numeric fingerprint of the image (a perceptual hash) that describes its visual structure. This fingerprint cannot be used to reconstruct the original image.
  • Reads basic post metadata already public on Reddit: post ID, permalink, author username, and submission time.

What the app stores

The app stores, per subreddit, for each image post:

  • The image's perceptual hash (a short string of characters, not the image itself)
  • The post's permalink, author username, and submission time

This data is stored using Devvit's built-in Redis storage, scoped to the subreddit the post was made in. The app does not store copies of submitted images. It does not access private messages, account credentials, email addresses, or any data outside of what's needed to compare newly submitted images against previous ones in the same subreddit.

What the app does with this data

Stored fingerprints are compared against new submissions to detect near-duplicate images. If a match is found, the app removes the new post and leaves a comment (and, depending on configuration, a notification to subreddit moderators) linking to the original post.

Data retention

Stored fingerprints and metadata are retained per the moderator-configured lookback window (default: 90 days) and are not shared outside of the subreddit's own moderation team.

Third parties

The app does not sell, share, or transmit any collected data to third parties. Images are fetched only from Reddit's own hosting and Imgur, solely to compute the perceptual hash described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the moderators of the subreddit where the app is installed, or via the app's Reddit developer page.
